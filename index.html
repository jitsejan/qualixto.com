What we do

End-to-end data platform delivery, from first architecture decision to production pipelines your team can operate without us.

Platform architecture

Cloud data lakehouse and orchestration platforms on AWS, Azure, or GCP: medallion architectures, domain-driven data products, and orchestration built to be handed over, not to create dependency.

Hands-on pipeline delivery

Production dbt models and Python pipelines with schema validation, contracts, lineage, and automated testing, written and shipped by us, not just designed on a whiteboard.

Governance & compliance

Data contracts, RBAC, classification, and audit-ready governance for regulated environments: banking, lending, investment management, and energy.

CI/CD & platform hygiene

Release process modernisation, GitFlow adoption, pre-commit standards, and branch protection across large multi-repo estates that have stalled or drifted.

AI-ready foundations

Governed data platforms built so LLM and analytics workloads have trustworthy, well-modelled data to sit on top of, plus direct experience shipping Bedrock and SageMaker-backed pipelines.

Mentoring & team building

Embedded engagements that grow junior and mid-level engineers alongside the platform: pairing, code review, and the delivery standards a team needs to own what we build together.
